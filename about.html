Entebbe police serves the comunity by promoting public safety,mataining law and order and protecting property

OUR HISTORY

Entebbe served as the original headquarters of the modern Uganda police force when it was established under the British colonial administration in 1906

Later Entebbe Police Station played a foundation role in modern community policing initiatives and specializd aviation problems for entebbe airport

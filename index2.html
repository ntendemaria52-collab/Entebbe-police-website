HISTORY OF ENTEBBE POLICE FORCE

Entebbe served as the original headquarters of the modern Uganda Police Force when it was established under British colonial administration in 1906.Origins and Headquarters (1906)Creation: On May 25, 1906, the Uganda Armed Constabulary was reorganized and renamed the Uganda Protectorate Police.First Inspector General: Captain W.F.S. Edwards arrived as the first Inspector General and based his headquarters in Entebbe.Administration: From Entebbe, Captain Edwards introduced formal systems of record-keeping, files, reporting, and statistics by 190

Station Development: By 1912, the expanding force grew to about 15 stations nationwide, incorporating specialized branches like the Criminal Investigations Department (CID) and traffic units.Capital Shift: Entebbe remained the central administrative hub of the protectorate and its security apparatus until Uganda gained independence in 1962, when the capital and primary government operations consolidated around Kampala.Local Legacy

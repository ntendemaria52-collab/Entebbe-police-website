PUBLIC DOCUMENTS

These are the documents the public can request from the police. Requirements and fees may change,so confirm with the nearest station before you go.

POLICE BOND

What it is:A written promise that lets a suspect be released from custody while the case contiues.police bond is free of charge.

Bring:The person's details,a guarantor(surety) with any documents the officer in charge asks for.

Where:The situation where the person is being held.

POLICE FORM 3(PF3)MEDICAL REPORT

What it is:A medical examination form used when someone is injured injured in an assualt,accident or domestic violence.

Bring:Your ID nad case refrence number from the station.

Where:Get the form from the police then get it filled

ACCIDENT REPORT

What it is: A report of a road accident, needed for insurance and court cases.

Bring: Your ID, driving permit, vehicle details, and the accident reference number.

Where: The traffic police office that handled the case.

LOSS REPORT

What it is: A record that you reported an item as lost, such as an ID, passport, or ATM card. Banks and offices ask for it when replacing items.

Bring: Your ID (or a copy) and a description of the lost item.

Where: Any police station.
